# Roadmap

Priority order: accuracy, reliability, security, functionality, performance, design.

1. Verification status per fault record (official manual / official source matched / third party / needs verification / conflicted / unsupported). Brand and model must be part of the match.
2. Fix database gaps: Deye W-codes, the 6 duplicate groups, records without a code. Only from official manuals, with source and page when known.
3. Find the official meaning of the Axpert "39 under FAULT" case before encoding anything.
4. Reading: crop to the display, glare handling, multi-code tables, confidence shown to the user.
5. Real-phone checks by the owner (capture, print, scroll) and then fixes.
6. Offline/caching tests and slow-network behavior.
7. Brand coverage: add brands from official manuals in small batches.
